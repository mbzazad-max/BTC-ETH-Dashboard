How reliable is this?

Range calibration

Ranges are sized from — past windows when BTC's volatility was at a similar level to now. Tested on the half of that history they were not fitted to, the 68% and 90% bands held — of the time for 1 week and — for 2 weeks. The 90% band is wider than a bell curve would give because BTC's rare big moves are bigger than average.

Volatility now

30-day realized — vs 1-year — (annualised). Daily ATR is in its — percentile. Historically, BTC's quietest stretches were followed by moves only — the usual size over 2 weeks: calm tends to persist, so don't bank on a squeeze.

What it can't see

Headlines, ETF flows, liquidation cascades and the exact FOMC outcome. The direction tilt is deliberately small; the range is the useful part. Re-check after each big event and after you refresh the CoinGlass snapshot.

A statistical outlook for planning, not financial advice.

How refresh works

  • Saved snapshot. Opening the page shows the last saved snapshot and makes no TradingView calls, so it loads instantly and stays inside TradingView's limits. The pill at the top says when it was saved.
  • Refresh from TradingView pulls the 4H, daily, weekly and monthly bars for BTC (Coinbase BTCUSD) and ETH (Binance ETHUSDT), four sentiment gauges and the US macro calendar one at a time, about 1.5 seconds apart, then recalculates every tab (ROI grids, Patterns, levels, spot plans, Forecast) for both coins and saves the result as the new snapshot. A source that fails keeps its previous data, and Fetch missing retries only those.
  • New candle. A yellow pill appears when a new 4H or daily candle has opened since the snapshot. The daily candle closes at 11am Sydney time.
  • Market sentiment. Refresh also pulls stablecoin dominance (USDT.D + USDC.D), BTC dominance and the alt market cap (TOTAL2) from TradingView. They feed the Market sentiment card and a signal in the Forecast.
  • CoinMarketCap Fear & Greed, Altcoin Season, market cap, dominance and US spot BTC and ETH ETF assets are pulled through your CoinMarketCap connector at the end of every Refresh (allow it the first time claude.ai asks).
  • CoinGlass funding, open interest and liquidations for BTC and ETH are read by Claude in your Claude desktop app (also in the daily 11:27am refresh). Say "Refresh the CoinGlass snapshot on my Bitcoin dashboard" in chat.
  • BTC / ETH switch. Every tab (ROI, Patterns, spot plan, Forecast, CoinGlass) follows the coin you pick; the market-sentiment gauges are shared. Your pick is remembered on this device.
  • Rules-based analysis for your own planning, not financial advice.
